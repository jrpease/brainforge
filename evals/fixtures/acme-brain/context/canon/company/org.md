# Org Design

## Teams and what they own

| Team | Owns | Lead |
|---|---|---|
| Core | the editor, the canvas, the file format | Rivera |
| Growth | onboarding, pricing surfaces, billing UI | Okonkwo |
| Platform | infrastructure, the public API, uptime | Bastian |

## Escalation

Anything customer-visible and broken goes to the owning team's lead first. If it is still
unresolved after **four hours**, it goes to the COO — not to the CEO, and not to a channel.

## Hiring

Teams cap at **eight** people. A team that needs a ninth splits instead of growing, and the
split is proposed by the team, not assigned to it.
