# Mission, Values & Operating Principles

## Mission

Put a working prototype in every designer's hands on day one.

## Values

- **Ship the smallest honest thing.** A half-feature that tells the truth beats a whole
  one that guesses.
- **Show the work.** Decisions carry their reasoning or they get re-litigated.
- **Default to written.** If it only exists in a meeting, it does not exist.

## Operating principles

- Every project has exactly one **directly responsible individual**. Consensus is an input,
  never an owner.
- **Two-week cycles**, with the last three days reserved for cleanup. No exceptions granted
  for "almost done".
- Any decision that costs more than **$25,000** or changes a customer-facing price goes to
  the leadership team before it goes anywhere else.
