# User archetypes

ACME has three archetypes. Maya the Maintainer runs the platform for a team of fewer than 50 and buys on a monthly plan. Devon the Deployer ships daily and cares about rollback speed. Priya the Procurer signs the annual contract and never opens the product.

Positioning is built around Maya: the one-line promise is "calm infrastructure for small teams". Priya is addressed only in pricing and security copy.
