# Pricing Model

Acme sells three tiers. Pricing is per-seat, billed monthly or annually, with
annual paid up front.

## Tiers

- **Starter** — $12 per seat per month. Up to 10 seats. Core editor, one project.
- **Pro** — $29 per seat per month. Unlimited seats, unlimited projects, shared
  component libraries, and the audit log.
- **Enterprise** — custom. Adds SSO, a dedicated success manager, and a
  contractual uptime SLA. Floor is 50 seats.

Annual billing takes 20% off the monthly rate on every tier. There is no free
tier; Starter carries a 14-day trial instead, and trials never auto-convert.

## Packaging rules

Seats are the only metered unit. We deliberately do not meter projects, storage,
or API calls — usage-based pricing punishes the exact adoption behavior we want
early in an account's life, and it makes the bill unpredictable for the buyer.

Features move down a tier when they stop being a reason to upgrade, never up.

## Discount policy

Sales may discount up to 15% without approval. Anything past 15% needs the VP
of Revenue. Discounts are granted as a percentage off list, never as a custom
rate card — custom rate cards make renewals unnegotiable.

Nonprofit and education pricing is a flat 40% off list on Starter and Pro, and
is granted by support rather than sales.
