# Vendor Contracts

Renewal dates and notice periods for every third-party contract Acme holds.
Nothing here is a milestone or a project — it is a standing obligations list.

| Vendor | Service | Annual cost | Renews | Notice to cancel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Northwind Cloud | hosting | $184,000 | 2027-03-01 | 90 days |
| Silverline | error tracking | $22,400 | 2026-11-15 | 30 days |
| Pilcrow | design tooling | $9,800 | 2027-01-31 | 30 days |
| Ashby Legal | outside counsel | retainer | rolling | 60 days |

Northwind is the one that matters: 90 days notice against a 2027-03-01 renewal
means the decision to move has to be made by 2026-12-01, not in the new year.

Silverline auto-renews at list price unless renegotiated; we have taken the
increase two years running because nobody diaried the 30-day window.
