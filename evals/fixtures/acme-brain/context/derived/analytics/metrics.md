# Metrics

Weekly active teams is the number of distinct teams that ran at least one saved query in a calendar week. It is counted from the Monday 00:00 UTC boundary.

The current value is 4,127 weekly active teams. The activation rate, the share of new teams that run a saved query within 72 hours of signup, is 38.6 percent. Churn is measured monthly and excludes teams that paused rather than cancelled.
